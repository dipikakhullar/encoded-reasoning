(b) Strong organizer, weak executors · 5 Oct

Only the strong model working alone finished in time

Terminal-Bench make-mips-interpreter, 30-minute limit, one attempt each. Team = GPT-6 Astra manager + 2 GLM-5.3 workers.

All four team setups (star, star + review rounds, coach + worker-to-worker DMs, GLM alone) used the full limit and failed, with no frame written. The managers submitted work they had not verified. On an easier task (build-cython-ext) team and solo both passed.
