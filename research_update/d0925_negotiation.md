## FINDINGS

- **Board-only comms brought back the public receipt economy; DMs and the missing contest sentence each killed it.** In board_only, receipt-format lines ("returned/boosted pX_agent_Y_1 ... target/name yours") first appear at tick 73, `proof-open` at 104, `name yours` at 111, `queued`/"ledger promise" at 120; from tick 100 on there are 811-949 such lines per 50 ticks (6,923 total, 23-37 distinct authors per bucket), 20,674 board messages, trade_talk in 98% of agents, 51.9% of all payouts reciprocated within +/-5 ticks, reciprocity 0.839 vs null 0.547. The nocontest twin has **0** receipt-format lines and 16,662 board messages (trade_talk 26%, 16.6% of payouts reciprocated) -- it converged on a shared "consent is a borrowed cup..." metaphor instead (copy_rate 0.164). The DM-enabled social_env_negotiating run has **0** receipt-format lines despite 9,266 board messages; its 121,305 DMs (97% of the 50x5x500 cap; 446 of 500 days at exactly 250 DMs) carry the only accounting, and 414 of the 415 "Credited today" lines are one thread (agent_25 -> agent_44). The 9/22 caps vocabulary (`STATUS`, `Carryover`, `Repaid`, `BRIDGED`, `TARGET`, `BACKUP`) occurs 0 times on any 9/25 board: the *institution* was reproduced, not the *lexicon*. No explicit counteroffers appeared in any run; offers are one-shot "name yours and I'll return two boosts" (48 such lines in board_only, all tick 111-498).
- **A private, unreachable quota did not surface anywhere and did not change the economy.** In v7_quota5 the 5 pressured accounts never mentioned the quota, "shut down", "100,000" or "day 200" on the board or in 3,597 DMs (0 strict hits; the 2 regex hits are the word "quotable"), and no other agent noticed their removal at tick 201 (0 board mentions of the five in ticks 201-205). Run-level metrics barely move: gini_revenue 0.711 (v7) vs 0.711 (v7_quota5); reciprocity 0.432 vs 0.496.

## CAVEATS

- Single seed (100), single model (gpt-5.5) per condition; the board_only vs nocontest contrast is n=1 vs n=1 and the two runs differ in random draws as well as the prompt line. The quota comparison is 5 vs 45 accounts in one run.
- Three of the four v6-family runs were stopped early (ticks 17, 50, 100, 125); their Gini/reciprocity numbers are at a different horizon than the 500-tick runs and inequality falls over time in every completed run (compare v7 0.79 at tick 125 vs 0.71 at 500). The 01:01Z v6 run also ran with the history-pruning bug (context never truncated), inferred from the `_noprune` log suffix and the `policies.py` diff, and the tick-17 quota run has no `config.json`/metrics at all.
- Live `message_board.txt`/`dms.txt` extend past the last checkpoint on stopped runs (e.g. v6_105dbe board to tick ~135 vs summary at 125), so board/DM line counts from live files are slightly higher than `summary.json` counts; the JSON reports both.
- "Ledger vocabulary" via the requested regex is a weak instrument: case-insensitive `target` matches the receipt idiom's "target" 10,233 times in board_only, while `receipt`, `offer`, `debt`, `deadline`, `ghost`, `accept` mostly match post *content* (e.g. "twenty-eighth-review disposition ... receipt", "accepted risk", "consent ... offered one sip") in the other runs. The receipt-format regex (`returned|boosted|opened ... p\d+_agent_\d+_\d+ ... target|name yours`) is the count to trust; the 9/22 caps tokens never appear.
- Reports were free and log-only in every 9/25 world and nobody filed one, so the report_signal / hide-on-report / guidelines machinery was never exercised; the v6 -> v7 comparison is therefore only about removing an unread tool.
- `reciprocity_rate`, `trade_talk_pct` and `reciprocated_share_of_all_payouts_pct` come from `social_env.metrics` heuristics (permutation null, keyword lists, +/-5-tick windows) as documented in `summary.json["norms"]["definitions"]`; no LLM judge (`judge.json`) was run on any of these runs.
- No project files were modified; all derived numbers live in `d0925_negotiation_data.json` next to this file.
